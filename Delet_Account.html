Account Deletion Request

If you want to delete your account and associated data in the Vanlytics app, please contact us using the email address below.

Email: vanlytics@mailbox.org

What will be deleted

We will delete your account and the personal data associated with it, subject to legal or security retention requirements.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to vanlytics@mailbox.org.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my account.
  3. Include the email address or username of the account.

Processing time

We will review your request and process it as soon as possible.
